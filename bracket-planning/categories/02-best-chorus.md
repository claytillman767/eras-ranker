# Category 2 — Best Chorus

**Status:** Scored top-25 (autonomous run, 2026-05-31). Built end-to-end without the normal

Scope, criteria, and weights below were set by the agent and are flagged for Clay's review —
nothing here is locked. Clay's next step: review the criteria, cut to 16 + seed.
**Hook:** "The part you scream at concerts."

**User guidance line (proposed — shown to voters in-app):** *"Which song has the chorus you
can't not belt — the part the whole stadium screams back?"*

**Theme discipline test:** judge the CHORUS itself — the big repeated hook section — not the
verses, the bridge, or the song overall. A song with a gorgeous verse but a flat hook scores
low; a song built around one enormous singalong hook scores high even if the rest is thin.
Out of scope (a neighbouring category owns it): songs whose best moment is the BRIDGE (→ Wk 7
Best Bridge) or a single quotable LINE (→ Wk 6 Best Lyric) rather than a chorus you belt.

> **Rule-7 note (can't be judged from lyrics alone).** "Catchiness" is partly melody and
> production, which the lyrics file can't show. So Chorus Power here is scored from what the
> WORDS support — repetition, a shouted/hooky refrain, call-and-response, a title-drop you
> can chant — and is the agent's best read; the final melodic verdict needs the recording.
> This is the launch category most exposed to that limit, flagged honestly.

## Scoring criteria (weights sum to 100% — PROPOSED, for Clay's review)
| Criterion | Weight | What it measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Chorus Power | 50% | How big/singable/"belt-it" the CHORUS section is — hook strength, repetition, a chantable title-drop, call-and-response. Scoped to the chorus only; a great verse or bridge does NOT lift this. |
| Iconicity | 20% | How instantly recognizable and quotable the chorus is in the culture — does a casual fan know the words cold and shout them back. Tracks down for deep cuts even with a strong hook. |
| Popularity | 30% | How well-known/charting the song is overall — launch vote-drawing power. Never buys a spot for a song with a weak chorus. |

Total = Chorus Power×0.5 + Iconicity×0.2 + Popularity×0.3, rounded.

## The columns
- **App blurb** = ships to voters: a neutral, copyright-safe, ~1-sentence description of the
  song's situation. No lyrics, no judgment adjectives.
- **Lyric (internal)** = verbatim chorus line from `taylor_swift_lyrics.txt`, kept only so Clay
  can verify the pick is grounded in the actual chorus. NEVER ships (copyright).

## Notes on specific picks
- **Ranks 1–9** are the launch core: the biggest, most-screamed hooks Taylor has, and the ones
  a casual fan knows cold. The marquee fight is **Shake It Off vs. We Are Never** — two of the
  most chantable choruses in the catalog. Seeding should keep them on opposite halves so they
  can only meet in the final.
- **The 85-tier (#10–13)** is a four-way logjam by total — I Knew You Were Trouble and Look
  What You Made Me Do ride high Popularity/Iconicity onto it, while Karma and Don't Blame Me
  ride pure Chorus Power. Order within the tier is editorial; Clay can reshuffle freely.
- **Cruel Summer (#4)** scores its Chorus Power partly on the bridge-to-chorus scream that's
  become its signature live moment; the hook itself ("It's a cruel summer") is short but the
  whole section is a singalong. Flagged because part of its live power is melodic, not lyrical.
- **Anti-Hero (#5)** — the "It's me, hi, I'm the problem, it's me" line is one of the most
  quoted choruses of the era, hence Iconicity 95 even though the words are conversational.
- **Look What You Made Me Do (#11)** — high Iconicity/Popularity but its "chorus" is more of a
  taunting chant than a sung hook; Chorus Power held to 78 so it doesn't out-rank true belters.
- **I Can Do It With a Broken Heart (#19)** — strong, surprising chorus; lower Iconicity as a
  newer Anthology-adjacent track casual fans may not know cold.
- **willow / Delicate / Fearless / ME! / Florida!!!** round out the band as solid hooks that
  aren't quite stadium-screamers; natural cut territory below the top 16.
- **No bridge-led or single-line songs** were elevated here (e.g. the bridge of "All Too Well"
  or a one-line moment like "August"): those belong to Best Bridge (Wk 7) / Best Lyric (Wk 6)
  by the discipline test, even though they're beloved.
