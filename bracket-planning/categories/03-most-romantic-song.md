# Category 3 — Most Romantic Song

**Status:** Scored top-25 (autonomous run, 2026-05-31). Built end-to-end without the normal
Step-1 user-guidance gate per Clay's explicit one-time authorization to run unattended.
Scope, criteria, and weights below were set by the agent and are flagged for Clay's review —
nothing here is locked. Clay's next step: review the criteria, cut to 16 + seed.
**Hook:** "The one you send without context."

**User guidance line (proposed — shown to voters in-app):** *"Which song says 'I love you' the
best — the one you'd send someone you're crazy about with no caption needed?"*

**Theme discipline test:** the song expresses warm, devoted, head-over-heels ROMANTIC LOVE
toward a partner — affection you'd want pointed at you. Out of scope (a neighbouring category
owns it): heartbreak and breakups (→ Wk 1 Best Breakup, Wk 5 Saddest), spite, anxious or
doomed situationships where the love is more fear than warmth, lust with no tenderness, and
one-sided pining where there's no actual relationship. A crush can qualify if it's sweet and
hopeful, but it scores below a song about secure, returned love.

## Scoring criteria (weights sum to 100% — PROPOSED, for Clay's review)
| Criterion | Weight | What it measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Romance/Devotion | 50% | How squarely the song is a warm, devoted expression of love for a partner — vows, tenderness, "I'm yours." Heartbreak, spite, fear-driven flings, and no-relationship pining score low. |
| Swoon Factor | 20% | How tender/heart-melting the ROMANTIC feeling lands — would it make someone melt. Scoped to the romance: emotion from a non-romance subject (nostalgia, anxiety, sadness) doesn't count, so it tracks down with Romance for off-theme songs. |
| Popularity | 30% | How well-known/singable the song is — launch vote-drawing power. Never buys a spot for a song that isn't actually romantic. |

Total = Romance/Devotion×0.5 + Swoon Factor×0.2 + Popularity×0.3, rounded.

## The columns

- **Lyric (internal)** = verbatim line from `taylor_swift_lyrics.txt`, kept only so Clay can

## Notes on specific picks
- **Ranks 1–7** are the clean romantic core and the launch's marquee fight: **Love Story vs.
  Lover** at the top — the fairy-tale proposal vs. the grown-up "you're my lover" vow. Both
  famous, both unambiguously about devoted love. Seed them apart so they meet in the final.
- **Two flavors of romantic** are deliberately both represented: *secure/devoted* love (Lover,
  Paper Rings, Mine, King of My Heart, Call It What You Want, Sweet Nothing, peace) and
  *falling/giddy* love (Enchanted, Begin Again, Fearless, Snow On The Beach, Sparks Fly, Hey
  Stephen, So High School). Both are romantic; the secure-love songs generally score a touch
  higher on Romance/Devotion because the love is returned and committed, not just hoped-for.
- **Mary's Song (#9)** is one of the most purely romantic songs in the catalog (a whole life of
  love), held off the very top only by low Popularity as an early deep cut — a deliberate test,
  like Tell Me Why in Best Breakup, of how hard fans punish an unfamiliar great fit.
- **The Life of a Showgirl** lands three entries (Honey, So High School, Opalite) — all
  genuinely romantic and worth giving the newest album visibility; Popularity capped because
  the album is recent.
- **Cruel Summer (#19) and Don't Blame Me (#25)** are flagged BORDERLINE: there IS real romance
  in them, but the feeling is anxious secrecy / obsession more than warm devotion. Cruel Summer
  rides its Popularity (90) onto the list despite leaning anxious-fling; natural cut territory.
- **gold rush, ivy, Wildest Dreams** were considered and held OUT of the top 25: gold rush is a
  daydream the singer won't let herself have, ivy is a guilt-laced affair, and Wildest Dreams
  is a romance she already knows is doomed — none is "send it without context" romantic.
